Using a phone’s hotspot?
  1. Turn on 2.4 GHz. The station cannot see 5 GHz. On an iPhone: Settings › Personal Hotspot › Maximize Compatibility. On Android: Hotspot › AP band › 2.4 GHz.
  2. Not in the list? Choose Other network and type the name exactly. An iPhone’s is under Settings › General › About › Name, with a curly apostrophe (’), which the phone’s keyboard types for you.
  3. Hotspot on this phone? Save here first. Then leave this network, turn the hotspot on and keep its screen open. The station joins within 30 seconds and the hotspot shows 1 connection.
