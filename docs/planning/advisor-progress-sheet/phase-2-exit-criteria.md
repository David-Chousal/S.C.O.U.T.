# Phase 2 Exit Criteria (Draft)

> **Summary** — Draft pass or fail criteria for Phase 2, System Integration (2026-10-22 to
> 2026-12-06), written from the Linear milestone descriptions so the advisor sheet has
> something measurable to score. **Draft: the team edits targets before the first sheet uses
> them.** Targets in brackets are not yet set.
>
> Part of the [Advisor Progress Sheet](README.md).

| # | Criterion | Target (pass when) | Lane | Linear milestone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Assembled buoy survives a drop | [drop height] with no leak or crack | GENG | Buoy proof tests |
| 2 | Assembled buoy self-rights | Returns upright from [angle] in a tank | GENG | Buoy proof tests |
| 3 | Assembled buoy floats in salt water | Freeboard within the model's ±0.5 in | GENG | Buoy proof tests |

| 6 | Sleep current | Measured below 5 mA | ECEN | Duty-cycle firmware |
| 7 | Two-D-cell power | 7 days on two D cells, extrapolates to ≥ 12 months | ECEN | Float soak and battery endurance |
| 8 | Sealed housing pressure test | Holds [depth or pressure] with no leak | GENG | Power and housing integration |
| 9 | Float soak | 4 weeks in salt water, with and without foam, no leak | GENG | Float soak and battery endurance |
| 10 | Ocean test plan | Site, permission, vessel and safety plan written and approved | GENG | Ocean test planned |
| 11 | Dashboard and analytics | Drift detection shown on the live dashboard | CSEN | Dashboard and analytics |

Criteria 1 to 9 are the ones the advisors will read as progress. Criteria 10 and 11 are
planning and software outcomes and should not outweigh them on the sheet.
