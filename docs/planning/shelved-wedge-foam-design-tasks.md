# Shelved Tasks: Wedge, Foam and Ballast-Stem Design

> **Summary** — Tasks put aside on 2026-10-16 when the battery-powered version moved to a printed-float
> buoy with printed infill (no foam, no ballast stem, no lead). They are kept here, with their Linear
> links, so they can be revived if the team goes back to the wedge, foam and stem design. Full task
> text stays in Linear: canceled and deferred issues remain readable and can be reopened.
>
> Part of the [planning docs](team-timeline.md). Why the design changed:
> [decision log, 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-16](../hub/decision-log.md).

## How to revive

1. Reopen the Linear issue (move it out of `Canceled` or `Deferred` to `Backlog` or `Todo`).
2. Remove the "superseded" note on the matching [decision log](../hub/decision-log.md) row.
3. Check the old numbers still apply: the wedge-design analyses are in
   [`docs/engineering/buoy-structural/`](../engineering/buoy-structural/) and were not rewritten.
