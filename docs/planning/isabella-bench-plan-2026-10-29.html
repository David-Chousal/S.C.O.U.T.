  4. Unplug → wire the SHT40: VIN → 3V, GND → GND, SDA → SDA, SCL → SCL. Plug in, read humidity.

Rules that prevent damage
  • Sensors only ever go to the Feather's 3V pin and GND. Never USB (5 V) or BAT. The M0's pins are 3.3 V only.
  • Unplug USB before changing any wire. Plug back in only after rechecking.
  • Never run the radio without an antenna. The buoy firmware transmits, so attach the antenna before loading it. Simple sensor test sketches are fine without one.

What could go wrong when you connect them

MistakeWhat happensHow you'd notice / fix
Probe red and black swappedDS18B20 runs backwards, gets hot, can die in seconds to minutesProbe warm to the touch → unplug at once. This is why the wires get labeled
Sensor on USB or BAT instead of 3V5 V on the SHT40 (rated 3.3 V) and 5 V signals into M0 pins can damage bothAvoided by only using the 3V rail
3V shorted to GNDThe regulator current-limits, the board gets warm, the laptop may cut the USB portCaught by the unplugged 3V↔GND check
Data wire in the wrong pin, or no pull-upNothing breaks; the reading just fails (e.g. −127 °C for the DS18B20)Move the wire, check the 4.7 kΩ
Radio on with no antennaTransmit power reflects back into the RFM95 and can damage itAntenna on before the buoy firmware

Everything here runs at 3.3 V from a USB port, so nothing can shock you, and the worst case is one cheap part. A spare DS18B20 is ≈ $10.

Antenna (bench)

  • Use the 915 MHz spring antenna (4269). Its straight leg goes in the ANT hole at the radio end of the M0; solder it from underneath.
  • Solder only the ANT hole, not the ground pads beside it. Keep the antenna straight and away from other metal.
  • A straight 78 mm (≈ 3 in) solid wire in ANT also works.
  • The uFL connector (1661) is for the outdoor antenna later. Ask Dr. Krishnan before adding it.

Getting data to your laptop

  • Sensors: no shore station needed. USB from the M0 to the laptop, then the Serial Monitor at 115200 baud prints every reading.
  • Radio test: plug the M0 and the shore station into the laptop. The shore window prints OK buoy=… temp_c_centi=… for each packet. No WiFi needed.
  • No SD card or coin cell needed. The firmware boots with sd=FAIL and the clock marked unset, and keeps going.
  • The 128 MB microSD (5250) was in the Adafruit order, so check that box. The CR1220 is in the Mouser box.
