## How an order works

1. John requests each item in **Quartzy**.
2. **Beverly Dutra** reviews it against the budget, approves it and places the order.
3. Beverly receives the delivery; the team picks it up from her.
4. If the proposal's hazards needed lab manager approval, the lab manager must first confirm the
   team is ready to begin. Not recorded whether ours did: ask Dr. Caldwell-O'Keefe.
