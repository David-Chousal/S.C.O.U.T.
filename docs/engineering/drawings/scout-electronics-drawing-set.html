Sheet 0 — Hydrophone selection: Aquarian H2dM

SCOUT-E-100 · SHT 0/7 · Rev B

Why this one

  • Fits the 3.3 V system: it runs on plug-in power, at least 2 V and 0.7 mA. 3.3 V through 1.1 kΩ gives about 2.5 V. No 5 V or 48 V rail needed.
  • Easy to wire: it has a standard 3.5 mm TRS plug. It goes into a jack breakout on the Proto board, so there is nothing to solder on the hydrophone itself.
  • Easy to read: the output is a plain analog voltage (1.1 kΩ output impedance). A two-stage MCP6002 preamp feeds the M0's ADC on A2. No codec chip and no I²S.
  • Good enough for reef sound: it covers below 10 Hz to above 100 kHz, which includes fish calls (50 Hz–3 kHz) and snapping shrimp (2–20 kHz) up to our 11 kHz sampling limit.
  • Rated well past our depth: under 80 m, against a reef depth of about 1–5 m. It weighs 105 g and measures 25 × 46 mm.
  • Good price: $229 base, the lowest-cost calibrated hydrophone we found that fits. The cable is 3, 6, 9 or 15 m; we are ordering 6 m (covers 3–6 m water depth).

Datasheet: H2dM user's guide, saved as DATASHEETS/aquarian-h2dm-manual.pdf. Order from aquarianaudio.com and confirm the cable-option price at checkout.

Sheet 6 — General notes, checks and references

SCOUT-E-100 · SHT 6/7 · Rev B

General notes

  1. All logic and sensors run at 3.3 V from the Feather's AP2112K regulator. No 5 V rail in the default build (MiniBoost 4654 is a backup only).
  2. Remove the Feather's MCP73831 charger before any primary lithium cell is connected. Bench testing uses a current-limited 3.6 V supply instead of the cells.
  3. Batteries connect in parallel through one ideal diode each, then the PTC fuse, then the keyed JST-PH plug.
  4. Turbidity board and hydrophone circuit are powered only while sampling (Q1 on D11, Q2 on A3, LOW = on; 100 kΩ pull-ups keep them off during sleep and boot).
  5. Hydrophone bias: 3.3 V through R9 100 Ω + R4 1.1 kΩ gives ≈ 2.46 V at the plug at 0.7 mA (minimum 2 V), matching Aquarian's test condition (2.5 V compliance, 1.1 kΩ).
  6. Expected hydrophone level: −172 dB re 1 V/µPa at a 120 dB re 1 µPa reef soundscape gives ≈ 2.5 mV; preamp gain ≈ 230 brings it to ≈ 0.6 V for the 3.3 V ADC †.
  7. Audio is stored on the SD card only; LoRa carries summary numbers (one 1-minute clip would need hours of airtime).
  8. Never transmit without an antenna attached. Deployed antenna gain must not exceed 2.15 dBi (RFM95 FCC grant).
  9. I²C addresses are unique (0x44 SHT40, 0x68 PCF8523). SPI chip selects: D8 radio, D10 SD; only one LOW at a time.
  10. Firmware polarity: config.h says the sensor gate is HIGH = on, but the P-FET switches here are LOW = on. Firmware must drive D11 and A3 LOW to power the sensors.
  11. DS18B20 data stays on D12 (firmware). The Rev A schematic label D5 is outdated; D5 stays the PCF8523 interrupt in firmware. D6 is left free in case the radio IO1 pad is ever jumpered for LoRaWAN.

Checked against the sources

  • H2dM: −172 dB, 1.1 kΩ output, 0.7 mA, min PIP 2 V, 3.5 mm TRS dual-mono (Aquarian H2dM manual p. 1–2)

  • DS18B20: 3.0–5.5 V, 4.7 kΩ pull-up, 750 ms (Maxim datasheet p. 6, 8, 19)
  • Grove turbidity: 3.3 V / 5 V, output falls as turbidity rises (Seeed wiki)
  • SHT40: I²C 0x44, 10 kΩ pull-ups on board (Adafruit guide)
  • Adalogger: RTC on I²C, SD CS = GPIO 10 on M0 (Adafruit guide p. 6–7)
  • Feather M0: BAT divider 100 k/100 k to D9 = AIN7; radio D3/D4/D8 (schematic rev B)
  • DMG2305UX: P-channel, −20 V, RDS(on) ≤ 100 mΩ at −2.5 V, VGS(th) −0.5 to −0.9 V, VGS ±8 V max (Diodes DS36196 p. 1–2, saved in DATASHEETS)

Build order (follows the Electronics Build and Test Plan)

  1. Stage 1: headers on the M0 and the Adalogger, coin cell in. On a breadboard: DS18B20 + R1, SHT40 via 4209, turbidity straight to 3V and A1. Get first readings over USB.
  2. Stage 2: measure sleep and active current with the meter in series.
  3. Stage 3: build Q1 + R2 + C1 + C2 on the breadboard and check that D11 LOW turns the sensor on. Then remove the Feather's charger.
  4. Stage 4: spring antennas on both radios; shore jumpers bridged. Range test.
  5. Stage 6: build the battery pack (7E), check its polarity, then the 7-day run.
  6. Stage 7: breadboard the hydrophone front-end (7D), check A2, record test clips.
  7. Stage 8: move all circuits onto the Proto board, terminals and jack at the edges. Thread cables through the glands before terminating. Fit the uFL → SMA path. Conformal-coat the boards (keep coating off the connectors and the SD slot), add desiccant, seal.
