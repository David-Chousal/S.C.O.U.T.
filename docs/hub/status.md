   Seven mechanical issues sit `Blocked` behind it: it gates housing dimensions
   ([SCO-49](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-49)), which gate the floatation buoyancy check
   ([SCO-48](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-48)), the FEA load cases
   ([SCO-73](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-73)), the chassis load path
   ([SCO-75](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-75)), stability
   ([SCO-80](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-80)), failure-case buoyancy
   ([SCO-81](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-81)), and the cap cable gland
   ([SCO-53](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-53)).
   [SCO-70](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-70)
2. **Power source — [ADR-0007](../decisions/0007-primary-cell-power-source.md)**, two primary lithium
   D cells with firmware standby, no solar. The direction was confirmed with the advisors on 2026-10-07
   but is **deliberately not final until the sleep floor is measured**
   ([SCO-23](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-23)), because whether two cells last about a year
   depends on that number. Until it's measured, the battery-bay size, firmware battery thresholds and
   the formal retirement of the old LiFePO₄ charging path
   ([ADR-0002](../decisions/0002-lifepo4-charging-path.md),
   [SCO-10](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-10), now `Blocked` on SCO-23) all stay open.
   [Power Budget Update](../engineering/power-budget-update-2026-10-05.md)
3. **Adafruit bench order placed 2026-10-07** (invoice 3758294, delivery est. 2026-10-13 to 10-16; Mouser turbidity-kit order placed the week of 2026-10-19, $81.82, delayed in shipping and expected by the week of 2026-11-02; [Purchase Log](../planning/purchase-log.md)) (Adafruit + Mouser, including the shore-station ESP32-S3 + LoRa FeatherWing; hydrophone, D cells and outdoor antenna are planned follow-up orders — [Electronics Build and Test Plan](../engineering/electronics-build-and-test-plan.md)). Until they arrive, firmware driver validation
   ([SCO-25](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-25)) and shore-station radio bring-up
   ([SCO-24](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-24)) are both written and waiting on hardware.
   The staged Rev A order (SCO-88) was cancelled 2026-09-24 in favour of one order covering the
   Scout and the shore station, placed after the Shoba design review
   ([SCO-116](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-116)).
   [SCO-115](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-115)
4. **Hydrophone** — *decided 2026-10-12:* Aquarian H2dM with a 6 m cable, on the 3.3 V rail through a 1.1 kΩ bias resistor ([Electronics Drawing Set](../engineering/electronics-drawing-set.md)). The purchase now waits only on funding (November).
   [SCO-8](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-8)
5. **Turbidity polarity must be confirmed on the bench** — the pipeline assumes a higher ADC
   count is clearer water; if the sensor read the other way, every downstream turbidity
   interpretation would silently invert. The prototype's Seeed Grove 101020752 runs at 3.3 V
   straight into A1 with **no front end** (2026-10-06), so the old SEN0189 non-inverting-divider
   concern ([SCO-47](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-47)) no longer applies; what remains is
   the bench check in [SCO-25](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-25). The Grove sensor itself is
   **not yet ordered** ([SCO-189](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-189)). *Updated 2026-10-23.*
6. **Rev A KiCad schematic puts the DS18B20 on the wrong pin** — it wires `TEMP_DATA` to
   `D5`, while `config.h`, `pin-assignments.md` and the Rev B drawing set all use `D12`. A board
   wired from the Rev A schematic never reads temperature; wiring from the Rev B drawing set
   avoids it. The standby wake no longer needs a `D5` wire: it runs on the SAMD21's own RTC
   since [SCO-165](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-165), so `D5` is free. Fix: update the
   KiCad schematic (ECE). [Integration Runbook §2, G3](../engineering/integration-runbook.md)
   *Updated 2026-10-25.*
