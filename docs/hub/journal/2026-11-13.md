## Changed 2026-11-13 — mechanical (John)

- **Electronics housing reprint failed too.** After the 2026-11-12 upside-down misprint
  ([journal](2026-11-12.md)), the reprint was printed with bed adhesion lowered on purpose, so the
  supports would be easier to remove afterwards. Adhesion was too low and the tree supports failed
  partway up the wall; the support interface is stringy and the wall is intact but the print is
  abandoned. Lesson: keep first-layer adhesion at the normal setting for tall tree supports, and
  trade ease of support removal for adhesion only on parts with short supports. Next step: raise
  adhesion and reprint; result not yet recorded.
