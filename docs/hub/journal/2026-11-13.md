## Changed 2026-11-13 — mechanical (John, [SCO-309](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-309))

- **Test Assembly 10 roll-decay CFD finished** (30 degree start, 6 s, fins meshed at 1.25 mm, run overnight on the
  Mac in two sittings). Period 0.824 s with fins and 0.822 s without, against 0.78 s from Capytaine. Fins case swing
  falls from 46.7 to 27.6 degrees (about 1.2 percent of critical damping); the no-fins case swing grows from 50.9 to
  63.9 degrees, which is numerical energy gain, so the fins-versus-no-fins gap (about 2 percent of critical at best)
  is not a clean measurement. Decision: keep the fins; the tub drop test with and without fins, from 10 and 30
  degrees, is still the reference. Results, plots and snapshots are in the
  [hull-hydrodynamics README](../../../mechanical/simulations/hull-hydrodynamics/README.md#test-assembly-10-multi-night-run-run-2026-11-12-to-2026-11-13).
