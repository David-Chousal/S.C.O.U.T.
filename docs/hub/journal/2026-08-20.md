**Phase:** 0 — Kickoff (2026-08-14 – 2026-09-04)

## Where the project stands

Mechanical design (John Ryan, `geng`) added two new parts to the bolted wedge floatation
assembly: a chassis cap and a wedge cap, modeled 2026-08-19. This is a first pass at the
[SCO-53](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-53) cable-gland cap revision — cable routing and
the cap's fit against the chassis are still open.

## Changed today

- **Chassis cap + wedge cap added to the bolted wedge assembly.** Wedge cap press-fits and
  epoxies into each wedge, sealing the foam cavity before fill. Chassis cap covers the central
  chassis cylinder and extends slightly over each wedge top — whether its OD is larger than or
  flush with the chassis is not yet decided. Planned build sequence: epoxy the wedge cap in,
  then inject foam — the gap under the chassis cap gives expanding foam somewhere to overflow,
  trimmed flush afterward (the same role flash plays in injection molding). Five new STEP files
  committed to `mechanical/cad/floatation/`, including the first CAD for the bottom cap under
  each wedge described in the 2026-08-17 snapshot but not modeled until now. Full narrative:
  [`mechanical/cad/floatation/README.md`](../../../mechanical/cad/floatation/README.md#chassis-cap--wedge-cap-added--2026-08-20).
- **Two new open questions on the same design:**
  1. **Cable exit routing** — a channel down the side of one wedge, or a direct through-hole in
     the middle of one wedge, for sensor/harness cables to exit through the chassis cap.
  2. **Solar mount attachment** — likely a direct screw-top mount to the chassis cap with
     clearance spacers, not yet modeled against the existing solar-mount bracket
     ([SCO-54](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-54)).
