## Where the project stands

One mechanical result today: the turbidity sensor-housing prototype, submerged for roughly a
week on a TPU-printed O-ring, was opened and found not waterproof.

## Changed today

- **Turbidity sensor housing — extended submersion failure.** John opened the housing after
  ~1 week submerged, sealed with a TPU-printed O-ring (a deliberate shortcut, not the specified
  AS568-137 ring). Not waterproof. Worth keeping the nuance rather than flattening it to a flat
  fail: the ring was, by his own assessment, very poor quality, and still kept out most water
  for most of the week — a slow-leak failure mode, not an immediate blow-out. Full record:
  [`mechanical/test/sensor-housing-tpu-oring-failure-2026-09-12.md`](../../mechanical/test/sensor-housing-tpu-oring-failure-2026-09-12.md).
- **Decision: no further housing submersion testing until a real, purchased O-ring is sourced.**
  John's reasoning — the printed ring's own inconsistency is the leading suspect for the leak,
  so continuing to test with printed rings would keep re-testing that same confound instead of
  the actual face-seal design. This is the second informal data point (after the
  [2026-08-24 bench test](../../mechanical/test/waterproofing-submersion-test-2026-08-24.md)) in
  the direction the original [O-ring manufacturing decision](../decision-log.md) (2026-08-17,
  off-the-shelf over printed) already assumed.
- **Corrected a stale claim in `facts.md`.** The housing-sealing-joint fact said "neither
  housing has been submersion-tested to this design" — no longer true for the sensor pod, which
  has now been tested (informally, with the wrong ring, and it failed). Updated in place rather
  than left to drift, per Standing Rule 1.
