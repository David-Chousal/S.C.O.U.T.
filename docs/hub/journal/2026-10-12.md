## Changed 2026-10-12

- **Electronics Drawing Set SCOUT-E-100 Rev B** added
  ([Electronics Drawing Set](../../engineering/electronics-drawing-set.md)). It covers:
  - the system block diagram and board stack-up;
  - the pin map and cable schedule;
  - material and equipment takeoffs;
  - a sheet listing every resistor, capacitor, wire and joint.
- **Sensor Interface Review SCOUT-E-110** for Dr. Krishnan (due 2026-10-14): datasheet
  screenshots, a block diagram per sensor, and the draft sensor test protocol.
- **Hydrophone decided: Aquarian H2dM with a 6 m cable.** It is biased from 3.3 V through
  1.1 kΩ (the manual's minimum is 2 V at 0.7 mA). The purchase waits for funding.
- **Pins:**
  - A3 is the hydrophone power switch and A2 the preamp output.
  - D12 stays the DS18B20.
  - D5 stays the clock interrupt.
- **Firmware polarity mismatch found:** the code drives the sensor gate HIGH = on, but the P-MOSFET
  switches are LOW = on. Logged for David as [SCO-186](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-186).
- **Purchase log updated:** required prototype electronics total about $502–652, hydrophone included
  ([Purchase Log](../../planning/purchase-log.md)).
