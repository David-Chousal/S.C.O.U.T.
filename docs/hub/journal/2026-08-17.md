## Where the project stands

Mechanical design (John Ryan, `geng`) closed two of the open decisions that had been sitting
under "Mechanical design" in [`status.md`](../status.md): O-ring manufacturing method and the
reef-safe anchoring/mooring approach. Both were made live in session rather than left for a
future PR.

## Changed today

- **O-ring manufacturing method decided** ([SCO-55](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-55)):
  purchased off-the-shelf standard rings, not the TPU-printed experiment or a batch-cast
  injection mold from PR #60. Printed parts are porous along layer lines — a
  waterproofing-critical risk at the 5 m pressure target — and standard rings have
  well-characterized long-duration compression-set behavior an untested printed part doesn't.
  Provisional: revisit if standard AS568 sizes don't fit the housing tolerances.
- **Reef-safe anchoring/mooring approach decided**
  ([ADR-0004](../../decisions/0004-reef-safe-anchoring-and-mooring.md),
  [SCO-17](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-17)): marked sites (existing pile/mooring)
  connect directly via line; unmarked sites get a single **mushroom anchor**, chosen because it
  sets by vertical embedment rather than dragging to bite, so deployment itself can't scrape
  adjacent coral. Sited adjacent to (never on) coral with swing clearance. Synthetic marine
  rope by default, chain in high-turbulence sites.
