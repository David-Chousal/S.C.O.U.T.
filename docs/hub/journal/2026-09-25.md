**Phase:** 1 — Subsystem Bring-Up (2026-09-07 – 2026-10-16)

## Where the project stands

The v5 buoy structure has now been checked in FEA and passes. The lean wedge bottom has a real
slicer weight, and every buoyancy number carries it.

## Changed today

- **v5 FEA re-check, 5 runs, all pass.** The lowest real buoy SF is ~3.5 (PETG), in the unfoamed
  thin wedge walls under the 5 m hydrostatic case. Ring buckling came out at load factor 1.87
  with no foam, which makes **foam fill structurally required** for the 5 m pressure test. See
  [FEA results § v5 re-check](../../../mechanical/test/fea-mooring-load-cases.md#results--v5-re-check-2026-09-25).
- **Lean wedge bottom sliced: 207.06 g** (4 walls, 0.18 mm layers, 15% gyroid, ~5 h 9 m). That
  is heavier than v4's 181.21 g. Nominal buoy 7.75 kg, reserve 210 N (3.77×), draft 2.53 in,
  freeboard 4.97 in, GM 9.56 in. The buoy is still substantially over-floated.

## Still open
