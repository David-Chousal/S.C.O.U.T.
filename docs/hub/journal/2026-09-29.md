**Phase:** 1 — Subsystem Bring-Up (2026-09-07 – 2026-10-16)

## Where the project stands

The buoy was found to float stably upside down, and the design now rights itself from any angle.
The v6 assembly and a committed stability calculator back that up.

## Changed 2026-09-28/29

- **Chassis prints upright** so the seal faces come out round (PR #152).
- **Electronics housing is flange-style**, and the flange caps the chassis. First-pass sizing:
  Ø4.375 in tube, Ø4.000 in opening, AS568-247 seal ([SCO-128](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-128)).
- **Stability rebuilt on STEP geometry.** The v5 buoy (3 in caps, no ballast): GM 7.1 in, vanishes
  at ~90°, **stable inverted**. Maximum hydrostatic righting (~4.8 N·m) is far below LC7 (~42 N·m),
  which contradicts §8 and is flagged for [SCO-82](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-82).
- **Fix decided:** 24 in ballast arm ([SCO-129](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-129)), 1.5 in
  caps ([SCO-130](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-130)), 316 pipe 4816K51 + 6040T57/T56 mount +
  backing plate ([SCO-131](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-131), [SCO-132](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-132)).
  v6 with 2.6 kg lead: 11.40 kg, freeboard 4.27 in, GM 12.8 in, self-rights from any angle.
- **Chassis print set:** 6 walls, 25% gyroid, 808 g ([SCO-133](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-133)).
- **v6 full assembly committed** ([`full-buoy-assembly-v6.step`](../../../mechanical/cad/full-buoy-assembly-v6.step)).
- **Calculator committed:** [`mechanical/simulations/buoy-stability/`](../../../mechanical/simulations/buoy-stability/README.md),
  with dated results and an interactive explorer.
