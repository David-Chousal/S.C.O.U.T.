## Changed 2026-11-03 — mechanical testing (John)

- **RR-23 Stage 1 failed.** The housing flooded: 1113 g with water, against 518 g dry. The three PG11 glands
  (with printed pegs) did not leak; the leak was the cap face seal, and the O-ring had no grease.
  [Record](../../../mechanical/test/rr-23-housing-cap-and-gland-seal-tests-2026-11-01.md).
- **Housing redesign:** the buoy mounting holes move out of the cap's outer diameter, so the cap becomes a
  uniform circle ([SCO-293](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-293)). Grease the O-ring next time.
- **Open contradiction:** John measured the O-ring as 5-7/8 in OD by 5-5/8 in ID; the records say AS568-247
  (ID 4.609 in). Logged in [`facts.md`](../facts.md), not reconciled.
- **RR-21:** the regular float read 5 3/8 in from the top to the water at 13:00 in salt water. John is
  dropping the mushroom float from the test. What was inside the float is not recorded, so the reading is not
  yet compared to the model.
- **Small test chamber CAD added** for testing the face seal alone (cable outlet removed, less plastic): groove
  5.625 / 5.975 in, 0.094 in deep, plain circular cap. [Housing README](../../../mechanical/cad/electronics-housing/README.md#small-test-chamber-for-face-seal-testing-2026-11-03).
