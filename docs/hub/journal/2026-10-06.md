**Phase:** 1 — Subsystem Bring-Up (2026-09-07 – 2026-10-16)

## Where the project stands

The bench electronics were ordered. The power plan settled on two D cells with firmware standby,
and the prototype became all 3.3 V. A staged build and test plan now ties every purchase to the
stage that needs it and the benchmark that proves it.

## Changed 2026-10-06

- **Parts ordered** ([SCO-115](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-115)):
  - **Adafruit, $142.58 incl. shipping and tax (invoice 3758294, ordered 2026-10-07; early buys: uFL + SMA panel cable, SHT40, JST battery plugs, FeatherWing Proto):** Feather M0 LoRa, Adalogger, DS18B20, microSD, antennas, stacking
    headers, the MiniBoost as a backup, and the ESP32-S3 + LoRa FeatherWing shore station.
  - **Mouser, ~$28:** Grove turbidity sensor + cable, CR1220, 2× DMG2305UX.
- **Power:** two Li-SOCl₂ D cells + firmware standby. The TPL5111 timer was dropped
  ([ADR-0007](../../decisions/0007-primary-cell-power-source.md), still 🟡 Open).
- **Turbidity:** the 3.3 V Seeed Grove 101020752 replaces the 5 V SEN0189 for the prototype.
- **Hydrophone direction:** the 3.5 mm plug-in-power version, not the XLR. The part number is
  still open ([SCO-8](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-8)).
- **Feather M0 schematic check:** confirmed the budget assumptions and corrected a gated floor
  to ≈ 55 µA because of the 100k pull-up on EN.
- **New:** [Electronics Build and Test Plan](../../engineering/electronics-build-and-test-plan.md),
  with 9 stages and their benchmarks.
