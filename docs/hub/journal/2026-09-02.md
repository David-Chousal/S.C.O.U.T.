## Changed today

- **Electronics housing sealing joint reworked into a static face seal**
  ([SCO-68](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-68)). Three changes together:
  - **Fasteners moved outside the O-ring boundary** — 6 bolts on a **Ø104.14 mm bolt circle**,
    ~6.2 mm radially outboard of the groove OD (Ø91.69 mm). No fastener pierces the sealed
    region. This is the direct fix for the panel review's *"fasteners currently sit inside the
    O-ring boundary, turning every one into a potential leak path."*
  - **Groove dimensioned to a standard AS568-043 ring** — ID Ø86.87 / OD Ø91.69 mm,
    **2.413 mm wide × 1.372 mm deep** → **22.9 % squeeze, 75 % gland fill**, both inside
    standard static-face-seal practice.
  - **Reinforced, fully-contacting seal faces** — the lands bottom out land-to-land at full
    assembly, so squeeze comes from geometry rather than bolt torque, and the flange resists
    bolt-to-bolt bowing that would otherwise relieve compression between fasteners.
- **Two new CAD files:** `electronics-housing-clamp-v2-body.step` (Ø114.3 mm OD × 118.11 mm,
  Ø101.6 mm bottom spigot, Ø78.74 mm top bore, the groove and the 6-bolt pattern) and
  `electronics-housing-clamp-v2-lid.step` (Ø114.3 × 6.35 mm, engraved identifying marking).
- **Printed, needs a reprint.** Slight O-ring tolerance issues on the first print. The offending
  dimension is not yet recorded.
- **Sensor pod sealed cap re-cut** — the pilot spigot went from **Ø31.75 → Ø31.496 mm**
  (0.010" off the diameter), now line-to-line with the body bore. Committed as
  `sensor-housing-sealed-cap-v2.step`. The pod **body** re-export is geometrically identical to
  what was already committed, so no new body file.

## Found by inspection, not reported

Three things came out of reading the STEP files rather than from John's account. All are
flagged in the CAD READMEs, none are resolved:

- **The lid has no clearance holes** for the clamp's 6-bolt pattern as exported.
- **Two incompatible sealing concepts now sit in the same folder** — this clamp and the older
  end-cap-slides-into-cylinder arrangement that `electronics-housing-body.step` is dimensioned
  for. Which one is baseline is undeclared.
- **The sealed-cap spigot change was not narrated** — whether it responds to a pod fit problem
  or is incidental is unconfirmed.

## Open questions raised

- **What does the lid's engraved marking actually say?** The STEP stores it as glyph geometry,
  not text, so the wording is not recoverable from the file — it needs recording from John so a
  future print is reproducible from the repo alone.
- **Does the AS568-043 free ID fit the Ø86.87 mm groove ID?** The squeeze and fill checks pass
  on cross-section; the diameter fit has not been checked against a supplier table.
- **Which O-ring tolerance failed on the print?** Capture it on the reprint so the groove
  numbers can be corrected rather than re-guessed.
