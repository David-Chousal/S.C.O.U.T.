## Changed 2026-10-28 — mooring load goes through the float

- **Decision:** the mooring load path for the dock/boat-hung V1 goes through the **float**, not the
  electronics housing. A sustained tether load on the housing would let the printed plastic creep
  and lose O-ring clamp compression over time, weakening the waterproofing seal. Recorded in the
  [decision log](../decision-log.md).
- **Consequence:** the printed top attachment
  ([SCO-243](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-243)) mounts on the float of both the mushroom and
  the wedge/ring buoys. The housing carries only its own weight.
- **Screening analysis behind it** (assumptions, not a test): a printed PETG eye is not the weak
  link. At a 250 N design load a 20 mm eye with a 6 mm hole, 6 mm thick, sees about 3 MPa, or about
  9 MPa with a stress concentration of 3, against roughly 15 MPa layer-direction strength. Print the
  loop flat in the layer plane and use a stainless shackle through it. The risk is the load path
  into the seal, which this decision removes. Proof-load test: [SCO-244](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-244).
