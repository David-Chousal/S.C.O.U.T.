## Where the project stands

Two sensor-pod mechanical items today: a sealing-method decision, then a formal packing
analysis that found the current CAD undersized — and, along the way, caught a stale
contradiction between two engineering documents that had disagreed since 2026-08-25.

## Changed today

- **Sensor-lead dry-chamber penetration: epoxy potting, not an O-ring.** Where the turbidity
  sensor's leads cross from the flood chamber into the dry chamber was never actually specified
  — the dry/flood split description and the O-ring manufacturing decision both predate an
  answer for this specific joint. John decided on epoxy potting, reasoning that an O-ring needs
  a smooth, round, dimensionally-precise mating surface that a cable simply isn't — a different
  failure mode than the printed O-ring that failed yesterday, but the same underlying lesson
  (seal method has to match the actual geometry being sealed). Requires an anti-wicking
  boss/sleeve, cable-jacket surface prep, and external strain relief so the pot isn't also
  doing strain-relief duty. Full write-up:
  [sensor-housing/README.md](../../mechanical/cad/sensor-housing/README.md#sensor-lead-dry-chamber-penetration--epoxy-potting-decided-2026-09-13).
- **First formal sensor-pod packing analysis — dry chamber is undersized.** Pulled the SEN0189's
  own manufacturer drawings (adapter board datasheet, plus a probe-body mechanical drawing
  fetched fresh from DFRobot's wiki and saved to `hardware/datasheets/`) and computed real
  chamber sizing for the first time. The probe's own **mounting ears** (44 mm diagonal span) —
  not the adapter board — govern the dry chamber's minimum diameter at **≥Ø52 mm**, bigger than
  the ~Ø31.5 mm bore already committed in the 2026-08-29 face-seal remodel. Also sized the
  probe-shaft penetration (Ø24 mm) and the flood chamber (≥Ø24 × 30 mm). Full analysis:
  [Sensor Pod Packing Budget](../../engineering/sensor-pod-packing-budget.md).
- **Caught and fixed a stale cross-document contradiction.** The
  [Electronics Housing Packing Budget](../../engineering/electronics-housing-packing-budget.md)
  (2026-08-25) had listed the SEN0189 adapter board as occupying volume in the *main* housing —
  silently disagreeing with the sensor-pod README's own "dry chamber holds the board"
  description the whole time. Confirmed with John: the board lives in the pod. Recomputed that
  document's three packing methods without it (Method 3's bare minimum: 68→63 mm,
  0.368→0.341 L) rather than leaving the wrong number standing now that it's been caught.
