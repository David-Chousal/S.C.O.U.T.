**Phase:** 2 — System Integration.

## Changed 2026-11-16 — console (David, [PR #344](https://github.com/David-Chousal/S.C.O.U.T./pull/344))

- **Dashboard Customize reworked into drag-and-drop** (branch `console/overview-every-metric`, not
  deployed). Each bay is one card with its width as fractions of the board (½, Full) over a compact
  sketch, so the whole board fits on one screen. Dragging a card shows a ghost under the pointer
  and a placeholder where it will land; bays also drag in from the Add a bay library; Escape
  cancels. The twelve-column background stripes are gone. Checked in local wrangler dev with
  seeded readings; not yet tried on live data.
- **Bay width back to drag-to-resize** (same branch, asked for by David): the ½ / Full buttons are
  gone; each card's right edge is a handle. Dragging it snaps live to the widths the bay allows and
  names the width while dragging; Escape puts it back; the arrow keys work on the focused handle.
- **Segmented controls match the keys beside them** (time range, Layout / Content, theme): still one
  joined group, but with the keys' height (including the small size in the Customize bar), text
  size, ink and bottom edge; the chosen choice is tinted and pressed in.
- **Fleet status table decluttered** (same branch, asked for by David): ten columns down to six (battery is left to the Lowest battery headline and Every metric).
  Firmware, uptime and restarts sit under each buoy's name; Link is "Last heard" with a dot and a
  word; the schedule shows "3 a day" and adds a status only when a command or settings change is
  pending or rejected; heat stress is a colour swatch and the level instead of a filled chip; flags
  are sentence case and wrap. No data was removed.
- **Housing seal tile** (same branch, asked for by David): the SHT40's %RH value stays on the SD
  card by design ([ADR-0005](../../decisions/0005-v1-sensing-payload.md)), so the Housing humidity
  tile never had data over LoRa. Power and housing now has a Housing seal tile read from the two
  packet flags: Sealed, Humidity high (the leak warning) or Sensor failed, since when, and how many
  readings in range ran high. The 70 %RH alert is still the placeholder awaiting
  [SCO-204](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-204).
- **Adjustable bay and tile sizes** (same branch, asked for by David): every bay can now also be
  given a height (bottom-edge handle in Customize → Content, 20 px steps, 120–2400 px, saved with
  the view; the worker validates it; double-click fits the content again). The Every metric tiles
  never get narrower than their content and each has a corner handle for width and height. Tile
  sizes are kept per browser, like the time range, not in the saved view.
- **Resizing can no longer hide information** (same branch): bay and tile heights are now
  minimums, so dragging up stops at what the content needs. Tiles snap to an even column grid with
  equal row heights. Heat stress lost its half width and Fleet its third width, because their
  tables did not fit; stored views snap to an allowed width. Checked in local preview with
  scripted drags at the pane's width (about 720 px of board).
