**Phase:** 2 — System Integration.

## Changed 2026-11-06

All on branch `console/fred-assistant` ([PR #295](https://github.com/David-Chousal/S.C.O.U.T./pull/295)), not deployed.

- **Console, Fleet:** each buoy has a ⋯ menu (Buoy info, Settings, Disconnect). A disconnected buoy's
  packets are accepted but not stored and no station is sent its settings; adding it again
  reconnects it; once disconnected it can be deleted with its settings and readings. D1 migration
  0013.
- **Console, Stations:** a ⋯ menu with Info (a card on what the station does) and Revoke, which now
  asks first and says how to bring the station back; deleting a revoked station asks first.
- **Console, Team:** a ⋯ menu with Reset link and Remove (disables the account; Restore undoes it);
  the "Signs in with" column is gone; invite roles show just their names, explained on hover.
- **Console, look:** white page instead of Tideline's warm stone; every dropdown in the menu's style
  and above every other layer; setting descriptions as delayed hover tips; the Firmware defaults
  table dropped from Settings; view-only notes in the top bar; a phone menu for Stations, Reports,
  Developer and Help; the sign-in page describes the console and centres its card on phones.
