## Changed 2026-10-16

- **Float fill.** The battery-powered version floats on printed infill, not foam. Foam sourcing is on hold ([SCO-166](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-166)).
- **Dry float.** The foam-version buoy floated well in a bin and leaked slightly ([SCO-170](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-170) Done).
- **Lead.** No lead ballast; the reef-permit check is canceled ([SCO-136](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-136)).
- **Corrosion.** Strategy decided: 316 stainless plus marine sealants and coatings ([SCO-92](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-92) Done).
- **Freeboard model.** [SCO-124](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-124) closed and pointed at the current model, the 2026-10-14 printed-float study: [`printed-float-tip-explorer/`](../../mechanical/simulations/buoy-stability/printed-float-tip-explorer/README.md) (~3.03 kg, 2.70 in draft, 3.80 in freeboard).
- **Shelved tasks.** Wedge, foam and ballast-stem tasks are canceled or deferred and listed in [`shelved-wedge-foam-design-tasks.md`](../../planning/shelved-wedge-foam-design-tasks.md).
- **Electronics housing seal.** Large AS568-247 O-ring now seals the housing; lid clearance holes cut ([SCO-107](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-107) Done).
- **Waiting on PETG.** [SCO-167](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-167) and [SCO-108](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-108) moved to Waiting on Parts. [SCO-190](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-190) in progress; external cables get a guide channel.
- **Monterey sanctuary reply.** MBNMS says a tethered buoy likely needs no sanctuary permit; anchoring needs an application. Open question back to us: what existing structure we tether to. See [Monterey Bay Sanctuary Permitting](../../research/monterey-bay-sanctuary-permitting.md).
- **Permitting route.** Path B chosen for Monterey and Hawaii: connect to an existing authorized structure, no anchor of our own. Hawaii starts with HIMB / CRIMP2; the other agency emails wait ([SCO-185](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-185)).
- **Deployment strategy.** New [Deployment Strategy](../../planning/deployment-strategy.md): Hawaii for reef data, Monterey Bay or Santa Cruz for systems and survivability tests only.
- **Slip sensor test.** Planned in Hawaii from John Ryan's boat in its yacht club slip, removed each outing; waiting on the club ([Deployment Strategy](../../planning/deployment-strategy.md)).
- **Permitting next steps saved** in [Deployment Strategy](../../planning/deployment-strategy.md#next-steps-for-permitting), with new issues [SCO-194](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-194) (yacht club) and [SCO-195](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-195) (Monterey / Santa Cruz host).
- **Float STEP v9.** Updated printed-float STEP added ([`printed-float-v9.step`](../../mechanical/simulations/buoy-stability/printed-float-tip-explorer/printed-float-v9.step)); includes the cable guide channel; float 992 g; re-run gives 2.87 kg, 2.56 in draft, 3.94 in freeboard ([SCO-190](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-190)).
- **FCC ID label engraved.** John Ryan engraved `Contains FCC ID: 2ASEORFM95C` into the electronics
  housing before the final print, satisfying the RFM95C modular-grant labelling condition
  ([SCO-103](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-103) Done). The STEP is committed as
  [`electronics-housing-engraved.step`](../../../mechanical/cad/electronics-housing/electronics-housing-engraved.step).
- **Sensor power-gate polarity fixed in firmware.** The sensor switches are high-side P-FETs, so gate
  LOW = on; the firmware had it backwards, which would have left sensors dark while reading and
  powered while asleep. `SENSOR_ON`/`SENSOR_OFF` added, both gates latched off before becoming
  outputs, A3 hydrophone gate and A2 preamp pins added, turbidity comments moved to the Grove
  101020752 ([SCO-186](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-186)). Bench check of the rail voltage
  still to do.
- **Transmit cadence decided: 3 fixed slots a day.** David adopted Isabella's SCO-165 plan over the
  firmware's one packet a day. The power budget already assumed 3 sends; the cost is ≈ 1% of the
  daily budget. Firmware changes with SCO-165
  ([SCO-196](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-196)).
- **Wake source decided: the SAMD21 RTC.** It runs from the Feather's 32.768 kHz crystal, so it is as
  accurate as the PCF8523 and needs no interrupt wire on Rev A; D5 is freed. The PCF8523 stays as the
  battery-backed wall clock, resynced once a day ([SCO-196](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-196)).
- **Slot schedule implemented in firmware (SCO-165, bench-unverified).** Samples at :00/:30;
  sends the latest reading at 00:10, 08:10 and 16:10 UTC; sleeps on the SAMD21 RTC and re-reads
  the PCF8523 after every wake. `PIN_RTC_INT` removed, D5 freed, D13 LED forced off. The Rev A
  `INT1` jumper is dropped from the integration runbook ([SCO-165](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-165)).
- **FCC review brief prepared** for Navid ([SCO-104](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-104)): eight
  questions with our current answers, in [FCC Review Brief](../../research/fcc-review-brief.md). A
  pre-review pass added the PSD check (≈ 19 dB margin), flagged the measured 6 dB-bandwidth margin
  as the weakest point, and corrected the §15.249 gap to ≈ 17 dB. New finding: the receive-only
  shore station is probably not bound by the 2.15 dBi antenna cap. Review itself not yet done.
