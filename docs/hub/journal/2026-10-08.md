## Changed 2026-10-08

- The Gantt replan (PR #163) merged: an MVP before winter break, target 2026-12-04.
- The sensor-pod cap no longer has a pilot spigot, so
  [SCO-109](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-109) was closed as moot and dropped from the
  Gantt. Recorded in the [decision log](../decision-log.md).
- **RR-15 failed**: the pod flooded completely with 6 bolts. The ring turned out to be a
  hardware-store ring with a 3/16 in (4.76 mm) cross-section, double the 2.62 mm the groove was
  cut for. A new body with a matched groove (about 28% squeeze) is registered as
  [RR-16](../../../mechanical/test/risk-reduction-register.md), not yet in the water.
- SCO-106 (O-ring fit) and SCO-139 (MVP benchmarks) closed; see the [decision log](../decision-log.md).
- Housing fixed at 4.4 in ID × 5 in tall ([SCO-49](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-49)),
  clearing the old FEA blocker. [SCO-73](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-73) is re-scoped to FEA of the new ballast stem (waits on [SCO-125](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-125)); the earlier LC1–LC9 runs stand.
  Chassis printed, mass/freeboard and stability finished, chassis load path done, foam covers the
  failure cases, cap cable gland merged into [SCO-68](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-68),
  Prusa XL dropped. See [`status.md`](../status.md) and the [decision log](../decision-log.md).
- Permitting researched for the Kaneohe Bay deployment, after Hannah Barkley's reply: see
  [Hawaii Deployment Permitting](../../research/hawaii-deployment-permitting.md). Lightest options
  are attended anchor-free tests and connecting to an existing authorized site; anything anchored
  needs the Corps first. Nothing has been confirmed by an agency yet; written determinations are
  the next step.
