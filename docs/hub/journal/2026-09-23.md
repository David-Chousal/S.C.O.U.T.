**Phase:** 1 — Subsystem Bring-Up (2026-09-07 – 2026-10-16)

## Where the project stands

Printer capacity is now the constraint on the flotation ring. The EPIC lab's medium Prusa
failed, the large lab printer is still offline, and a full wedge bottom takes over a day to
print. Six are needed.

## Changed today

- **Medium Prusa failed, and is running on a workaround.** Its filament feeder had already been
  bypassed before today. John bypassed it again and put a rolling filament stand inside the
  enclosure. It appeared to work, but it isn't a proper repair.
- **Wedge bottom split into two parts** in CAD so each half fits and finishes on a Prusa Mini.
  It is one clean radial cut down the center, from the outer radius to the inner radius,
  giving two 30° halves with a flat butt joint. The joint method isn't recorded yet, and the
  halves' STEP isn't committed.
- **First half started on the Prusa Mini.** The bottom layers were poor: some nozzle drag, and
  filament-feed resistance at one point under-fed the bottom-layer infill. John left it running
  and will check it on 2026-09-24. See the
  [print log](../../../mechanical/test/wedge-bottom-split-print-2026-09-23.md).
- **Advisor meetings with Jes Kuczenski set tentatively for Tuesdays at 12:15.**
- **Hoeseok Yang (ECEN) recorded as a third faculty advisor** (from the 2026-09-22 team
  charter draft). README and CLAUDE.md updated.
