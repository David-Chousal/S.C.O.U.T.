## Changed 2026-11-15 — console (David, [SCO-314](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-314))

- **Heat stress on the console**, decided in [ADR-0017](../../decisions/0017-console-heat-stress.md).
  The Dashboard has a heat-stress bay (also in the built-in Overview): each buoy's NOAA Coral
  Reef Watch alert level, DHW, daily mean and MMM; for one buoy, the daily-mean temperature over
  12 weeks against its MMM and MMM + 1 °C, and DHW over 12 weeks against CRW's Alert Level floors.
  The same numbers are in `/api/v1/heat-stress`, a new "bleaching alert at or above" email rule
  and Fred's `get_heat_stress`.
- **The MMM comes from NOAA**: Settings → Buoys takes the buoy's position and looks up CRW's
  5 km cell, MMM = CRW SST − CRW HotSpot (David's choice); it can be typed in instead. Kāneʻohe
  Bay's cell gave 26.87 °C.
- **Same science as the Python**: `console/worker/heat-stress.js` is a port of `bleaching.py`,
  checked against a fixture the Python generates. One stated difference: a console day needs half
  of its 1–3 transmits, not half of 48 samples.
- **Surfaced, not reconciled**: `bleaching.py` calls a day with no data "No Stress"; the Liu 2014
  note says to use the CRW virtual station's MMM, the console uses the 5 km cell. A stale line in
  `sources.md` (pipeline "stops at Level 2") was corrected.
- **State**: built and tested on the branch, checked in local wrangler dev; ⚠️ not deployed, D1
  migration 0017 not applied to the live database.

## Changed 2026-11-15 — firmware (David, [SCO-224](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-224))

- **Transmit slots are offset per buoy**: every slot moves ((`buoy_id` − 1) mod 4) × 30 s later
  (`TX_SLOT_OFFSET_STEP_S`, `TX_SLOT_OFFSET_POSITIONS` in `config.h`; `scout_tx_slot_offset_s` and
  `scout_offset_tx_slots` in `lib/scout_scheduler`), so the two buoys planned per shore station
  never transmit at the same second. SCOUT-01 keeps 00:10 / 08:10 / 16:10.
- The shore's ±2 min window is unchanged and covers all four offsets (last packet ends by +92 s).
- Unit-tested (no two of four buoys share an on-air turn; every offset packet ends inside the
  window); ⚠️ never run with two buoys on air.
