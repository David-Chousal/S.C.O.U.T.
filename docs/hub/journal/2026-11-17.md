**Phase:** 2 — System Integration.

## Changed 2026-11-17 — shore station and bench link (Isabella, [SCO-155](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-155))

- **First bench run of the whole chain**: bench buoy (M0) → LoRa → ESP32-S3 shore station →
  WiFi (phone hotspot) → console. Written up as
  [RR-32](../../../hardware/test/rr-32-shore-link-and-console-bench-2026-11-17.md), **Partial**.
- **Shore wiring settled**: the FeatherWing works on CS 10 / RST 11 / IRQ 9 (runbook G8). The
  wing is joined to the S3 by 8 jumpers, not stacked: header pins on the CS/IRQ/RST pads stood
  taller than the stacking headers. 915 MHz coil-spring antennas on both boards.
- **On the board for the first time**: the phone setup page (`SCOUT-Setup-2DD4`), WiFi join,
  NTP time and the console push. Station `shore-1-bench` and buoy SCOUT-01 show Live.
- **Numbers**: 16 of 16 frames valid with no `seq` gap, one a minute, RSSI −28 to −40 dBm on one
  desk; every frame pushed (`0 queued`). Breath test: `HUMIDITY_HIGH` on two frames, cleared on
  the next, shown on the console.
- **Not yet**: loss over 100+ frames, TX current, range; the shipped shore build (ran the
  example-key `_downlink` build, real `downlink_key.h` / `ota_pubkey.h` asked of David on
  [PR #351](https://github.com/David-Chousal/S.C.O.U.T./pull/351)); BroncoFi
  ([SCO-304](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-304)).
- **Tooling**: `set_rtc_windows.ps1` (bench handoff folder) set the M0 clock on its first hardware
  run; it needs `-Port` when the S3 is also plugged in (both are Adafruit USB IDs). The S3 upload
  needed the manual bootloader (hold BOOT, tap RESET).
- **Next**: demo of the chain on the console for Shoba (2026-11-18), then the 100+ frame loss run.
