**Phase:** 1 — Subsystem Bring-Up (2026-09-07 – 2026-10-16)

## Where the project stands

Isabella finished the deployment power budget and reviewed it with Dr. Shoba Krishnan. The bench
parts order is placed with Shoba after the review (~1:30 PM). The priority is now making the
system run on D cells for a year.

## Changed 2026-10-05

- **Power budget written up** as a dated draft:
  [Power Budget Update — 2026-10-05](../../engineering/power-budget-update-2026-10-05.md). The
  board's sleep floor (≈ 501 µA) is ≈ 90% of the ≈ 13.3 mAh/day draw; the hydrophone schedule
  (≈ 13.4 min/day) adds ≈ 16.9 mAh/day.
- **Working power decision (not final):** a 3.6 V Li-SOCl₂ primary D cell with no solar, TPL5111
  power-gating between wake-ups, and two D cells as the fallback. Recorded as 🟡 Open in
  [ADR-0007](../../decisions/0007-primary-cell-power-source.md)
  ([SCO-10](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-10)).
- **Fire safety:** the Feather's onboard LiPo charger is removed on the deployment board, the cell
  gets a one-way element, a fuse and a keyed disconnect, and the bench uses a current-limited
  supply instead of a cell.
- **Parts order** ([SCO-115](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-115)): Feather M0 LoRa,
  Adalogger, DS18B20, SEN0189, 5 V boost, TPL5111, plus switching parts. **Not ordered:**
  hydrophone, solar panel, LiPo/PID 6106.
