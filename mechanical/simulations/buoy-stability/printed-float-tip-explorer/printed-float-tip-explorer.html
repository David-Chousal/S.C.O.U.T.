Printed-float buoy: tip explorer

Drag the heel slider (or press Play) to tilt the buoy. The waterline stays level; G is the center of gravity and B the center of buoyancy. When B swings out past G on the low side the buoy rights itself; if it swings the other way it keeps going over. Hull from the committed float STEP, masses are estimates.

AssumptionsFloat chambers sealed and dry; no foam. Electronics masses from the repo budget stacked from the housing bottom up (housing 385 g, boards 176 g, glands 58 g, antenna 30 g). Mooring hardware 220 g at the keel; hydrophone element 150 g beside the pod. Ballast sits just above the keel inside the hull, with no buoyancy credit. Hull is treated as a body of revolution with the housing lid flange above the float top. Not covered: mooring heel, waves, flooding, biofouling.
