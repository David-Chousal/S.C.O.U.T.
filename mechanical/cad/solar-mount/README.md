# Solar Mount

CAD for the solar panel mounting structure — a 4-arm cross bracket with a central ring that
fits around the electronics housing chassis, positioning the solar panel(s) at the top of the
buoy near the antenna/LoRa equipment (see
[Sensor Housing → System context](../sensor-housing/README.md#system-context)).

## Components

| File | Onshape source | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [`solar-mount.step`](solar-mount.step) | `Initial Frame > Solar Mount` | Early concept, from the same initial whole-system pass as the early stem/floatation/body concepts — largely superseded elsewhere in this repo, but this is currently the only solar mount design captured |

Geometry: four flat arms extending from a central ring sized to the chassis cylinder — likely
intended to spread panel-mounting area without adding significant windage or weight at the top
of the buoy. Not yet refined into a current/final iteration the way floatation and electronics
housing have been.

**Native source:** see [`mechanical/cad/README.md`](../README.md#native-source) — one Onshape
document covers the whole project, not a separate one per subsystem.
