## Part A — Plan


### A1. Purpose and risk retired
Does raw, uncoated PETG take up a measurable mass of water over 24 hr, fresh then ~35 ppt salt? Retires the risk that absorption lowers the waterline and clouds leak tests.

### A2. Hypothesis
If raw PETG is submerged for 24 hr, then its mass gain is no more than the scale resolution, because PETG is a low-absorption plastic; the buoy's cavities may add trapped water, which the coupons separate out.
