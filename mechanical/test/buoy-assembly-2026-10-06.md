# Buoy Bottom Sections and First Glued Assembly — 2026-10-06

> **Summary** — John printed new bottom sections of the buoy and assembled everything built so
> far: wedges, chassis and bottom caps. The inside seams were glued with Gorilla Glue, and the
> assembly is taped to hold position while it cures. Marine epoxy goes on the outside once the
> glue has cured. Build record, not a test; no result yet.
>
> Part of [`mechanical/test/`](README.md). Related: [SCO-93](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-93)
> (chassis print), [SCO-75](https://linear.app/scout1/issue/SCO-75).

## What was done (John Ryan, 2026-10-06)

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Printed | New bottom sections of the buoy |
| Assembled | Wedges, chassis and bottom caps, as built so far |
| Inside seams | Gorilla Glue (a polyurethane glue) |
| Outside seams | Marine epoxy, **after** the glue cures |
| While curing | Blue painter's tape over the top and down the sides to stop the parts shifting |
